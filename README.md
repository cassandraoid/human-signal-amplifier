# Human Signal Amplifier

An agent skill for Claude and other AI agents that helps you
identify, articulate, and amplify your irreplaceable human value
in an AI-driven world.

**Not reassurance. Not generic categories. A specific, honest
profile of where your human signal lives — and language you
can actually use. Built to grow with you over time.**

**The initial diagnostic takes under 15 minutes.**

## What this skill does

The Human Signal Amplifier works in two modes:

**Mode 1 — Initial diagnostic**
An 8-question intake run conversationally, one question at a
time. Produces a complete Human Signal Profile with:

1. Your human signal named specifically — not a category,
   a statement that could only describe you
2. Where it shows up — two or three concrete contexts
   you'll recognize immediately
3. How to make it more visible — one or two specific
   behavioral changes
4. Language you can actually use — sentences built for
   your specific audience and context
5. A post lead — an opening line for LinkedIn or professional
   sharing, written in your voice

**Mode 2 — Living system**
Return anytime to add an experience, log a decision, capture
a moment where your signal was visible. The skill processes
it, connects it to your existing profile, and confirms what
it adds.

Every 90 days — or when your context shifts significantly —
request a profile refresh. Your accumulated logs synthesize
into an updated profile with stronger evidence, sharper
language, and a new post lead.

**Your signal gets stronger every time you use it.**

## The five signal dimensions

The skill maps your answers to five dimensions where human
signal actually lives:

- **Contextual judgment** — the call that requires reading
  this specific room, not the 80th percentile answer
- **Relational trust** — accumulated over years,
  non-transferable, cannot be generated by a model
- **Reasoning under ambiguity** — when there is no right
  answer in the data and someone has to be accountable
- **Pattern recognition from lived experience** — the map
  built from your specific path, especially the non-obvious parts
- **Human presence** — what you transmit that changes how
  people feel and therefore how they act

Most people have one or two that are clearly strongest.
The skill identifies those — it does not give you all five.

## Who this is for

Anyone whose value is being questioned — by the market, by
their organization, or by their own internal narrative — in
a moment when AI is making that question louder.

That includes:
- Professionals navigating a role change, reorg, or job search
- Job seekers who don't know how to answer "what do you
  bring that AI can't?"
- Consultants and advisors whose clients keep asking
  why they need them
- Leaders trying to articulate what the humans on their
  team are for
- Founders pitching in a world where every investor
  asks about AI moats
- Knowledge workers who feel their work could be automated
- Anyone in transition trying to understand what they
  actually are

The 8-question intake works for all of them. The output is
specific because it's built from their answers — not from
a category they selected.

## The living system

Most positioning tools are one-time assessments. The Human
Signal Amplifier is designed to grow with you.

Your human signal isn't static — it compounds. Every
experience you have, every decision you make, every moment
someone comes to you specifically adds to the map. But most
people don't capture those moments. They happen, they pass,
and the learning dissolves.

The living system changes that. Log experiences as they
happen — a sentence is enough. Return every 90 days for
a profile refresh. Over time you build something no
resume or LinkedIn profile can replicate: a specific,
evidenced record of your irreplaceable human capability.

---

## Design philosophy

An amplifier doesn't change the signal — it makes it
stronger without distorting it.

You are not being told who to be. You are being helped
to make what you already are more visible and more
legible to the people who need to see it.

The non-obvious path is almost always where the strongest
signal lives. The unconventional background, the industry
change, the experience outside the mainstream — these are
not gaps to explain away. They are the origin of the
judgment, pattern recognition, and presence that makes
someone hardest to replace.
