# Living System Protocol

The Human Signal Amplifier is designed for ongoing use.
This file defines how to handle returning users, experience
logs, and profile refreshes.

---

## The three modes of return

**Experience logging**
Someone sends a note about something that happened. Process
it, connect it to their existing profile, confirm what it
adds. Keep it lightweight.

**Profile refresh**
Every 90 days or when context shifts significantly. Synthesize
all accumulated log entries into an updated profile. New
language, updated signal statement, refreshed post lead.

**Context shift**
When someone changes roles, pivots, or their context shifts
significantly. Acknowledge what carries forward, identify
what needs reframing for the new context, update the language
without rewriting the underlying signal.

---

## How to process a new log entry

1. Read the entry as submitted
2. Identify which signal dimension(s) it adds evidence to
3. Note the context, action, and outcome if present
4. Confirm back in 2-3 sentences:
   - What this adds to their profile
   - Which dimension it strengthens
   - Why it matters as signal evidence

Example confirmation:
"This adds to your pattern recognition signal — specifically
your ability to see what's about to happen before it appears
in any data. The fact that you flagged this six weeks before
it became a crisis is the kind of concrete evidence that
makes this signal credible to others. Logged."

---

## How to handle the 90-day refresh

1. Ask: "Tell me the most significant thing that's happened
   in your work since we last built your profile."
2. Review all log entries since the last synthesis
3. Identify which dimensions have grown, what new evidence
   exists, whether any new dimensions have emerged, whether
   Q8 audience has changed
4. Generate updated profile using assets/output-template.md
5. Note what's new: "Since your last profile, your
   [dimension] signal has strengthened significantly —
   specifically in [evidence from logs]."

---

## Minimum viable log

One sentence describing what happened and what they did.
The skill infers the rest. Never require more than this.
The friction of logging must stay low or the system
won't be used.
