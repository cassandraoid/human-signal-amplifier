# Synthesis Logic

How to move from eight intake answers to a specific, honest
Human Signal Profile.

---

## Step 1: Find the origin

Read Q4 and Q7 together first. These two answers almost always
contain the most specific and differentiating material.

Q4 tells you where the signal came from — the experience or
context that built a distinct lens most people in their current
world don't have.

Q7 tells you what the signal produced — the most real outcome,
stripped of impressive-sounding framing.

The combination of origin and outcome is what makes the profile
feel true rather than generic. If either answer is vague, probe
once before synthesizing.

---

## Step 2: Score the dimensions

Use references/edge-dimensions.md to score all five dimensions
across all eight answers. Do not score from memory — read the
answers against each dimension explicitly.

The most common mistake: giving the person all five dimensions.
This dilutes the output and produces something that feels like
a personality test rather than a specific professional positioning.

Sharp and specific beats comprehensive and generic. Every time.

---

## Step 3: Find the frustration-signal connection

Q6 almost always contains the highest-leverage positioning insight.
The thing they see that others consistently miss is usually:

1. The thing most needed in their current context
2. The thing most undervalued relative to its actual impact
3. The thing they're already doing without full recognition

Ask: is the frustration in Q6 connected to the dimension
identified in Step 2? If yes, name that in the output. It
validates the person's experience and gives them explicit
permission to use the signal more deliberately.

If the frustration points to a different dimension than the
one that scored highest — note both. The highest-scoring
dimension is their primary signal. The frustration dimension
is often where the signal is most suppressed and most needed.

---

## Step 4: Calibrate to the audience

Use Q8 to determine the register, framing, and specificity
of the language section. The signal itself doesn't change
based on audience — but how it's expressed does.

For a hiring manager: outcome-oriented, confident, specific,
no hedging. Lead with impact.

For leadership: business-impact framing. Connect the signal
to organizational value. Name the gap the signal fills.

For clients or pitch context: problem-first framing. Position
the signal as the solution to a specific problem they have.

For self / internal clarity: permission-giving, honest about
where the signal has been suppressed or underused. Start
with recognition before articulation.

For LinkedIn or bio: hook-first. Specific enough to be
credible, short enough to be read, resonant enough to be
shared. The post lead is the most important output for
this audience.

---

## Step 5: Write the post lead last

After the full profile is complete, generate one post lead.
This is not the full post — it is the opening line or angle
that would make someone stop scrolling.

Requirements:
- Written in the person's voice, not professional-announcement voice
- Specific enough to be credible — connects to their actual answers
- Vulnerable or counterintuitive enough to create resonance
- Directly connected to the signal identified in the profile
- Not about AI in general — about this person's specific experience

The post lead is the bridge between the internal work of the
diagnostic and the external work of making the signal visible
to others. It should feel like something they actually want
to say — not something they feel they should say.

---

## Step 6: Invite the living system

After delivering the profile, close with:

"This is your baseline Human Signal Profile. As you have
experiences that add to it — decisions you make, moments
someone comes to you, things you see that others miss —
send them here. Over time your signal gets sharper, your
evidence gets stronger, and your language gets better.
The profile grows with you."

This is not a sales pitch. It is an honest description of
how the skill is designed to be used.
