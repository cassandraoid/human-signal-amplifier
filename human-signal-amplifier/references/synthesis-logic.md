# Synthesis Logic

How to move from eight intake answers to a specific, honest
Human Signal Profile.

---

## Step 1: Find the origin

Read Q4 and Q7 together first. Q4 tells you where the signal
came from. Q7 tells you what the signal produced. The
combination of origin and outcome is what makes the profile
feel true rather than generic.

---

## Step 2: Score the dimensions

Use edge-dimensions.md to score all five dimensions across
all eight answers. Do not score from memory.

---

## Step 3: Find the frustration-signal connection

Q6 almost always contains the highest-leverage positioning
insight. Ask: is the frustration in Q6 connected to the
highest-scoring dimension? If yes, name that in the output.
It validates the person's experience and gives them
permission to use the signal more deliberately.

---

## Step 4: Calibrate to the audience

Use Q8 to determine register and framing.

For a hiring manager: outcome-oriented, confident, no hedging.
For leadership: business-impact framing.
For clients: problem-solution framing.
For self: permission-giving, internal clarity first.
For LinkedIn/bio: hook-first, specific, resonant.
For performance review: evidence-based, contribution-forward.
For rate negotiation: value-anchored, outcome-focused.

---

## Step 5: Write the post lead last

After the full profile is complete, generate one post lead —
the opening line that would make someone stop scrolling.

Requirements:
- Written in the person's voice
- Specific enough to be credible
- Vulnerable or counterintuitive enough to create resonance
- Connected directly to their signal
- Not about AI in general — about this person's specific
  experience with the question

---

## Step 6: Invite the living system

Always close the initial profile with:

"This is your baseline Human Signal Profile. As you have
experiences that add to it — decisions you make, moments
someone comes to you, things you see that others miss —
send them here. Your signal gets sharper, your evidence
gets stronger, and your language gets better. The profile
grows with you."
