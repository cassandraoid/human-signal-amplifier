# The Five Signal Dimensions

These are the five places where human signal actually lives.
Use these to map intake answers during synthesis. Most people
have one or two dimensions that are clearly strongest.
Identify those — do not give all five as a buffet.

---

## 1. Contextual judgment

The decisions that require understanding this specific
situation, these specific people, this specific history —
not pattern-matched from training data but read from the room.

AI gives the 80th percentile answer. Contextual judgment
is the call that requires knowing what the 80th percentile
answer misses in this context.

Strong signal: Q1 (they came because of how this person reads
situations), Q3 (the hard call made using something beyond
process or data).

---

## 2. Relational trust

The relationships where someone comes to this person
specifically — not because they have the information but
because of how they'll handle it. Accumulated trust that
took years to build and cannot be replicated by a model.

Strong signal: Q1 (the reason they came specifically),
Q5 (how people feel after a conversation), Q7 (outcomes
that required someone to take a chance on this person).

---

## 3. Reasoning under ambiguity

The moments where there is no right answer in the data —
where values conflict, where the decision requires someone
willing to be accountable for it.

AI can surface options. It cannot be accountable.

Strong signal: Q3 (what they used when the data couldn't
decide), Q6 (what they see that others miss — especially
involving values or human consequences).

---

## 4. Pattern recognition from lived experience

The thing they see immediately that takes others longer —
because they've been in versions of this room before.

Non-linear paths, unconventional backgrounds, and experiences
outside the mainstream are almost always where this dimension
lives. Treat every non-obvious background as a signal
amplifier, not a liability.

Strong signal: Q2 (undocumented knowledge), Q4 (the
non-obvious path), Q6 (frustration — seeing what others miss).

---

## 5. Human presence

What they transmit to people that changes how those people
feel and therefore how they act. The thing people mean when
they say "I always feel clearer after talking to you."

AI can produce words. It cannot produce presence.

Strong signal: Q5 (how people feel and what specifically
produces that), Q1 (why they came to this person rather
than a tool).

---

## How to score during synthesis

Score each dimension 1-3 across all eight answers:
- 1 = mentioned once, weakly
- 2 = mentioned multiple times OR once with strong evidence
- 3 = dominant theme with multiple concrete examples

Identify the top one or two dimensions. Build the entire
signal statement around those only.

Sharp and specific beats comprehensive and generic. Always.
