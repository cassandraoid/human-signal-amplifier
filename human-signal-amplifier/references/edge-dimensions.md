# The Five Signal Dimensions

These are the five places where human signal actually lives. Use
these to map intake answers during synthesis. Most people have one
or two dimensions that are clearly strongest. Identify those — do
not give all five as a buffet.

---

## 1. Contextual judgment

The decisions that require understanding this specific situation,
these specific people, this specific history — not pattern-matched
from training data but read from the room.

AI gives the 80th percentile answer. Contextual judgment is the
call that requires knowing what the 80th percentile answer misses
in this particular context with these particular people.

Strong signal in the intake: Q1 (they came to this person because
of how they read situations), Q3 (the hard call made using
something beyond process or data).

---

## 2. Relational trust

The relationships where someone comes to this person specifically —
not because they have the information but because of how they'll
handle it. The accumulated trust that took years to build and
cannot be replicated, transferred, or generated by a model.

AI can simulate rapport. It cannot accumulate the specific trust
someone has placed in a specific person over time.

Strong signal in the intake: Q1 (the reason they came specifically),
Q5 (how people feel after a conversation), Q7 (outcomes that
required someone to take a chance on this person).

---

## 3. Reasoning under ambiguity

The moments where there is no right answer in the data — where
values conflict, where stakeholders have competing legitimate
interests, where the decision requires someone willing to be
accountable for it.

AI can surface options and model scenarios. It cannot be
accountable. It cannot hold the weight of a decision that
affects real people in real ways.

Strong signal in the intake: Q3 (what they used when the data
couldn't decide), Q6 (what they see that others miss — especially
when it involves values, ethics, or human consequences).

---

## 4. Pattern recognition from lived experience

The thing they see in a situation immediately that takes others
longer — because they've been in versions of this room before
and recognize the pattern from the inside.

Not book knowledge. Not training data. The specific map built
from a specific path. The person who navigated poverty and now
works in wealth. The one who changed industries three times.
The one who grew up in a context that the people around them
have only read about.

Non-linear paths, unconventional backgrounds, and experiences
outside the mainstream are almost always signal, not gap. The
most distinctive pattern recognition almost always comes from
having been somewhere most people in the current context
haven't been. Treat every non-obvious background as a
signal amplifier, not a liability to explain.

Strong signal in the intake: Q2 (undocumented knowledge),
Q4 (the non-obvious path), Q6 (the frustration — seeing
what others miss).

---

## 5. Human presence

What they transmit to people that changes how those people feel
and therefore how they act. The thing people mean when they say
"I always feel clearer after talking to you." The ability to
hold a conversation that makes someone feel seen, understood,
or capable in a way they weren't before.

AI can produce words. It cannot produce presence. It cannot make
someone feel genuinely understood. It cannot be the person
someone calls when they need to feel less alone in a hard decision.

Strong signal in the intake: Q5 (how people feel after a
conversation and what specifically produces that), Q1 (why they
came to this person specifically rather than a tool or a search).

---

## How to score during synthesis

After completing the intake, score each dimension 1-3:
- 1 = mentioned once, weakly or in passing
- 2 = mentioned multiple times OR once with strong concrete evidence
- 3 = dominant theme with multiple concrete examples and outcomes

Identify the top one or two dimensions — those scoring 3 or
strong 2s. Build the entire signal statement around those
dimensions only.

A sharp single signal is more useful than a comprehensive
but generic profile. When in doubt, go sharper.
