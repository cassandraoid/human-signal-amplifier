# Log Entry Template

Minimum: one sentence describing what happened.
Maximum: 3-5 sentences covering what, how, and outcome.
Never require more than this.

---

## Quick log (minimum viable)

"[What happened and what you did.]"

Example:
"A founder came to me before making a hire because they
trusted my read of the candidate more than the interview
scores."

That is enough. Send it. The skill will do the rest.

---

## Full log (most useful for 90-day refresh)

Date: [approximate is fine]
What happened: [1-2 sentences]
What you did: [what you brought to it]
What you used: [instinct, pattern, relationship, lived
experience, values — not process or data]
Outcome: [what happened as a result]
What this might add to my profile: [optional]

---

## Prompts to help you log

- Someone came to me specifically this week for...
- I made a call that the data couldn't make for me when...
- I saw something coming that nobody else seemed to notice...
- Something frustrated me because it was obvious to me
  but not to the room...
- I was in my element when...
- The moment I felt most useful this week was...
- Something happened that reminded me why my path matters...
