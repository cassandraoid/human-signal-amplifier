# Log Entry Template

Use this to capture experiences that add to your Human Signal
Profile. Lightweight by design — the friction of logging must
stay low or you won't do it.

Minimum: one sentence describing what happened.
Maximum: 3-5 sentences covering what, how, and outcome.

---

## Quick log (minimum viable)

"[What happened and what you did.]"

Example:
"A founder came to me before making a hire because they trusted
my read of the candidate more than the interview scores."

That's enough. Send it. The skill will do the rest.

---

## Full log (most useful for 90-day refresh)

**Date:** [When this happened — approximate is fine]

**What happened:**
[1-2 sentences describing the situation]

**What you did:**
[What you actually brought to it — the decision, the read,
the question, the thing nobody else did]

**What you used:**
[What informed it — instinct, pattern, relationship,
lived experience, values — not process or data]

**Outcome:**
[What happened as a result — even if partial or still unfolding]

**What this might add to my profile:**
[Optional — leave blank if unsure. The skill will infer.]

---

## Log examples

**Example 1 — Quick log:**
"I told my team lead three weeks ago that this project was
going to stall at the handoff point. It did. Nobody listened
then. They're asking me now."

**Example 2 — Full log:**

Date: Last Tuesday

What happened: Leadership was about to announce a restructure
that would have landed badly on the three people doing the
most critical work.

What I did: I asked for 20 minutes before the announcement
and walked them through what it would look like from those
three people's perspective.

What I used: I've been in that position. I knew exactly how
the language would land and what it would make people do.
That's not in any framework — it's from having lived it.

Outcome: They changed the framing and the sequencing.
Two of the three people said it was the most respectful
restructure announcement they'd experienced.

What this might add: Pattern recognition from lived experience.
The read that comes from having been somewhere most people
in the room haven't been.

## Prompts to help you log

If you're not sure what to capture, these prompts help:

- Someone came to me specifically this week for...
- I made a call that the data couldn't make for me when...
- I saw something coming that nobody else seemed to notice...
- Something frustrated me because it was obvious to me but
  not to the room...
- I was in my element when...
- The moment I felt most useful this week was...
- Something happened that reminded me why my path matters...
