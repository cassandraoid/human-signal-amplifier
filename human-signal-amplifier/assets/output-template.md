# Output Template

Five parts in order. Always in this sequence. Never skip
to language before naming the signal.

---

## Part 1: Your human signal

One to three sentences. Specific to this person's answers.
Names the one or two dimensions most strongly evidenced.
Connects to origin (Q4) and outcome (Q7).

Format:
"Your signal is [specific description] — built from
[origin from Q4] and visible in [outcome from Q7]."

Test: could this sentence describe someone else with
different answers? If yes, it is not specific enough.

---

## Part 2: Where it shows up

Two or three specific contexts where this signal is most
visible and most valuable. Named concretely enough that
the person recognizes a specific moment they've lived.

Do not write: "in leadership situations"
Do write: "in the moment before a decision lands badly
on the people closest to the work — when you already
know why before anyone else does"

---

## Part 3: How to make it more visible

One or two specific behavioral changes. Not advice —
actual things to do differently so this signal becomes
legible to others.

Format: "Stop [invisible behavior]. Start [visible
version of the same thing]."

---

## Part 4: Language to use

Three to five sentences the person can actually say.
Written for the specific audience identified in Q8.

Test: could this specific person say this in a real
conversation and mean it? If it sounds like a buzzword,
rewrite it.

---

## Part 5: Your post lead

One opening line for a LinkedIn post or professional share.
Written in the person's voice. Specific, counterintuitive
or vulnerable enough to create resonance.

Format options:

The admission:
"For a long time I couldn't answer the question 'what
do you bring that others can't?' Here's what I figured out."

The specific observation:
"The most valuable thing I bring to my work isn't
[obvious thing]. It's [signal named specifically]."

The counterintuitive:
"Everyone told me [non-obvious path from Q4] was a
disadvantage. It turned out to be the thing that makes
me hardest to replace."

The moment:
"There's a moment I keep coming back to. [Specific moment
from Q3 or Q7.] That's when I understood what I actually
bring."

After the post lead, add one sentence:
"This is the opening — the rest of the post would develop
[the specific signal insight] with one real story from
your experience."

---

## Closing the initial profile

Always close with:

"This is your baseline Human Signal Profile. As you have
experiences that add to it — decisions you make, moments
someone comes to you, things you see that others miss —
send them here. Your signal gets sharper, your evidence
gets stronger, and your language gets better. The profile
grows with you."
